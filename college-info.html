Admission Process

Follow these steps to complete your admission into the program:

  1. Submit the online application form with academic transcripts.
  2. Complete the entrance assessment or qualification review.
  3. Attend the counseling and department interview session.
  4. Verify original documents and complete fee payment for enrollment.
